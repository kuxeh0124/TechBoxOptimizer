GitHub-ready project

Optimizer

Primary objective is maximum resonance. Balanced mode then spends only the allowed resonance-loss budget to improve the selected/nearest Twinborn path. Every Epic chest is allocated somewhere, including future banking when it does not improve resonance immediately.

Run the optimizer after loading or editing your inventory.

Screenshot inventory import

Upload several inventory screenshots from the same inventory screen. The parser adds all detected cards together, detects owned Legend Twinborns, replaces the normal inventory, and recalculates.

The recognizer is calibrated from your three supplied screenshots. Purple, Epic, Legend, all 12 tech types, and the six current Legend Twinborns are supported. New UI themes, unusual crops, and future Eternal card artwork should be reviewed manually until more calibration examples are added.

Twinborn ownership

This is deliberately separate from normal tech inventory. Clearing normal inventory does not clear your Twinborns. Missing Twinborns become first-class progression targets.
