Resonance + Eternal Twinborn Optimizer

The same loss budget is used by Balanced mode when choosing where your Epic chests should go. If the Twinborn goal is already funded, spare chests return to the normal resonance path.
Important: Epic/Legend ingredients are consumed when merged. A current Legend Twinborn is assumed to be dismantled only at the final Eternal craft, returning one Legend Lv0 of each component. Until then, those two component pieces are treated as locked inside the Twinborn and are not counted as resonance support.

Resonance energy values

Purple pieces have no resonance value here, but they are still included because they can legally merge upward into Epic.

Twinborn ownership

These checkboxes are separate from your normal tech inventory. Clearing inventory will not remove them. If a Twinborn is missing, Auto mode first banks Epic0 chests toward making that Legend Twinborn; once owned, its roadmap switches to Eternal progression.

Twinborn-aware chest banking

A chest that is not needed for today’s maximum resonance is now treated as future capital. V8 treats resonance and Twinborn progression as a constrained tradeoff. Balanced mode first finds the absolute maximum resonance for your chest count, then permits only the loss you explicitly allow and spends that headroom to push the selected/nearest Eternal Twinborn as far as possible. Pure Resonance ignores Twinborn progression; Twinborn Rush prioritizes Twinborn funding regardless of the loss budget. This costs no resonance by itself because the app does not force you to merge the parked Epic parts. If a Twinborn is missing, V8 funds its Legend pair first. Once owned, V8 separately checks whether the later Eternal craft is worth the resonance tradeoff.

Eternal Twinborn roadmap

For an owned pair, “chests to fund” assumes you keep the Legend Twinborn intact until the final Eternal craft, then dismantle it and reclaim one Legend Lv0 of each component. For a missing pair, the first goal is simply one Legend Lv0 of each component to create the Legend Twinborn. “Resonance after craft” is only shown for an owned, fully funded Eternal Twinborn.
